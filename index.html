01 Workflow

Three steps to
automated distribution

STEP 01
📁

Add your videos

Drop your music video clips into the watched folder. The app recognises each clip, matches it to your song catalogue, and queues it automatically.

STEP 02
📅

Schedule is generated

A posting schedule is built automatically — 9:30am and 8:30pm daily. Captions, hashtags, and first comments are selected based on each song's mood category.

STEP 03
🚀

Posts go live

At the scheduled time, the video uploads to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts simultaneously — at full original quality with no manual input required.

03 Connect

Authorise your TikTok account

To enable automatic publishing, connect your TikTok account. The app requests only the permissions it needs.

  • 📤
    video.publish Upload and publish original music videos to your TikTok account
  • 👤
    user.info.basic Verify your account identity during the login flow

Your credentials are stored locally on your device and never sent to any third-party server. Revoke access anytime from TikTok settings.
